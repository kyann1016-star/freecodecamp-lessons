Chocolate Cake Recipe

Indulge in a rich, moist, and utterly delicious homemade chocolate cake that is quick to prepare and perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 1 and 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 and 1/2 teaspoons baking powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x13 inch baking pan.
  2. Mix all the dry ingredients together in a large bowl, then whisk in the wet ingredients until smooth.
  3. Pour the batter evenly into the prepared baking pan.
  4. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
